Geofence to geofence trips

A trip starts when the vehicle leaves the From geofence (last reading inside) and ends when it next reaches the To geofence (first reading inside). If it goes back into From first, the trip restarts from there. When From and To are the same, it shows how long and how many km the vehicle took to come back to that geofence. km = GPS km between the two readings; stops on the way = stopped or idling on the road. Very short hops (under 10 min and under 0.3 km, GPS jumping at the edge) are left out. Geofences drawn later count for past data only after select public.rebuild_segments(48);.

Loading pairs · tipper ↔ excavator

A loading is counted when a tipper stands (stopped or idling) for at least two readings next to an excavator or loader whose engine is on. The excavator's position is the median of its last 20 minutes, so GPS jumps do not move it. When two excavators are close, the nearer one gets the loading. For the first 7 days every excavator uses 50 m; after that its radius is set automatically to the distance within which 90% of its loadings happened (admin can override below).

Loading rules and learning

A tipper's next loading counts only when it has travelled at least the minimum trip km since its previous counted loading. A shorter visit is the same trip: it is not counted again and its km are carried into the next trip. A loading also needs the tipper to stand next to the loader for at least the minimum loading time. The tables show how the real data of the last 14 days is spread, so these limits can be tuned. Suggested values appear once there are 30+ real trips.

Radius learning

P50 / P90 = distance within which half / 90% of the excavator's loadings (last 7 days) happened. Learned radius is set once a day after 7 days and at least 15 loadings, between 15 m and the learning radius. A manual value (admin) always wins; clear it to go back to automatic.

Machine utilization

Machines are the vehicle types listed in Settings (Excavator, Loader, Roller…). Engine on = engine running (a machine working in one place at under 3 km/h counts as engine on). Running = time spent moving. Machine efficiency = Engine on ÷ total time passed in the selected period × 100 (Today: hours passed since 12:00 AM; Yesterday: 24 hours; 7 days: the last 168 hours). Off = engine off; No signal = GPS not sending. Time is counted from the 2-minute readings, so gaps when the poller was down are left out. Loadings come from the Loading pairs page.
